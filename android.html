Straight answers.

Does it block ads in the YouTube app?

No. The YouTube app can't be filtered on a phone that isn't rooted. Share a video to AshtaLok's private browser instead, and it plays there without ads.

Is AshtaLok a VPN?

It uses Android's VPN setting so it can run on your phone, but your traffic isn't sent to an AshtaLok server and your location doesn't change.

Does it work on mobile data and Wi-Fi?

Yes. The filter runs on the phone, so it works the same on both.

Will it break apps?

Occasionally. Pause protection, allow that site, or run the guided check to find exactly what to allow.

Does it block every ad?

No blocker does. AshtaLok blocks ads and trackers from the servers on its lists and yours, and shows you each block.

Does it block phishing sites?

Yes, at every level. AshtaLok blocks the sites on PhishTank’s list of verified phishing pages, the fake sign-in and payment pages it confirms are still online. Services many people share, such as Google Docs or Dropbox links, are never blocked as a whole. Checked on Android 15 with live phishing sites.
