Straight answers.

Does it work in Safari?

No. Safari isn't supported.

Can I install it now?

Not yet. It isn't in any extension store. The download page will link to it once it is.

Does it block every ad?

No blocker does. It blocks requests to the ad and tracker servers on its lists.

Does it block phishing sites?

Yes, at every level. The extension downloads PhishTank’s list of verified phishing pages itself, at most once a day, and blocks those sites even on a site you’ve paused. Services many people share, such as Google Docs or Dropbox links, are never blocked as a whole. Checked in Chrome.
