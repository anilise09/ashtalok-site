The AshtaLok mascot

Meet Rook, the wall-warden.

Rook stands on the wall and decides what gets through. Cool, unhurried and hard to impress: ads get a raised palm, and everything else gets a nod.

The AshtaLok mascot

Rook

The one on top of the wall, deciding what gets through.

Calm, sharp, a little dry. Rook has seen every trick an ad server can pull and isn't impressed by any of them, but never makes you feel silly for asking. A pro at the gate, friendly off duty.

Why it works: A silhouette of its own: the long flared coat with square shoulder plates, a brick stand-collar in the logo's navy-to-teal, and a glowing teal visor that still reads as a line of light at 32 px. The raised flat palm is the signature move. No hood, no mask, no borrowed emblems.

How we got here: three hero concepts

Earlier concepts Mortie, Hush and Parry

Candidate 1 · The wall itself

Mortie

A pocket-sized castle wall with a face on its middle brick.

Cheerful, steady and a little bit stubborn. Mortie is the wall your ads stop at, and is quietly proud of it.

Why it fits: Ties straight to the tagline. The battlements and brick courses give it a silhouette of its own, and the teal bricks echo the logo.

Candidate 2 · The quiet watcher

Hush

A calm, round-eyed owl who notices trackers so you don't have to.

Unhurried, observant, never alarmed. Hush sees everything and says very little, which is exactly the point.

Why it fits: A quiet hunter, as AshtaLok's protection is meant to be. Two-pronged ear tufts and a heart-shaped face disc keep it owl, not cat, even at small sizes.

Candidate 3 · The round sentinel

Parry

A little round robot on a tread, parrying ads with a brick-crest shield.

Upbeat and quick on its feet. Parry treats every blocked ad like a well-timed save.

Why it fits: Pale round body, blue dome, off-centre amber antenna, visor and tread base give it its own silhouette. The shield carries the brick motif so it still says 'wall'.
